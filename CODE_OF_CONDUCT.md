# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

Participants are expected to:

- Communicate respectfully and constructively.
- Focus discussion on technical substance.
- Assume good intent and ask clarifying questions before escalating.
- Accept and apply feedback in review.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Personal attacks or hostile behavior.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Repeated off-topic disruption.

## Scope

This applies to all repository spaces, including issues, pull requests, and discussions related to this project.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove content and may block participation for behavior that violates this policy.
